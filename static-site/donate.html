Support Rural Micro-Industries in Bangladesh

Your donation helps equip villagers with the training, tools, and financing to build sustainable micro-industries in their own communities — international donors welcome.

How does my donation help? Bongshai Foundation nurtures micro-industries in agricultural production, food processing, mini textiles and craft industries, and local storage and sales — through training, tools, and community support in rural Bangladesh.
