Our Development Programs

Hands-on training, technology transfer, and capital support for rural Bangladesh.

Target Region: Rural Bangladesh Last Updated: August 18, 2026
What programs are offered by Bongshai Foundation? Bongshai Foundation provides four primary programs: Agro-Engineering & Farming, Food Processing & Manufacturing, Mini Textiles & Handicrafts, and Micro-Industry Finance & Market Access, supplemented by community health and education initiatives across Bangladesh.

What Micro-Industry Programs Does Bongshai Foundation Operate?

Every program is engineered to build practical, revenue-generating capabilities within rural villages.

Agro-Engineering & Sustainable Farming

Comprehensive vocational courses in agricultural machinery maintenance, high-efficiency irrigation, scientific fish culture, and livestock management (broiler and sheep farming).

Food Processing & Manufacturing

Setting up localized processing hubs for potato chips, fruit juices, and dried foods. Trainees master food safety standards, preservation, and commercial packaging to add value to raw harvests.

Mini Textiles & Artisan Crafts

Revitalizing indigenous craft industries and decentralized garment assembly. We provide sewing machinery, pattern cutting training, and quality control mentorship for women and youth.

Micro-Industry Finance & Market Access

Delivering seed funding and equipment financing to graduate entrepreneurs, while establishing local storage and direct supply chain links to major consumer markets.

What Specific Technical Skills Are Taught in Our Workshops?

Practical curricula designed to turn unskilled labor into certified technical specialists and workshop owners.

Agro-Equipment Operations

Operation and field repair of power tillers, diesel water pumps, threshers, and automated seeders.

Aquaculture & Bio-Farming

Fingerling management, pond aeration, organic fish feed preparation, and sustainable hatchery techniques.

Commercial Food Preservation

Dehydration technologies, pasteurization, sanitary food handling, and shelf-life extension methods.

Supply Chain & Bookkeeping

Basic accounting, inventory storage management, cooperative governance, and wholesale price negotiation.

Bongshai Foundation

A social development initiative dedicated to grassroots rural economic empowerment and micro-industry incubation across Bangladesh.
